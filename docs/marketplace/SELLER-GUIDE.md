# Seller guide — CV Studio Design Marketplace

## Earn

You set the price ($4.99–$49.99). After payment fees, **you keep 70%** (platform commission 30%).

## Steps

1. Apply + complete Stripe Connect
2. Build template in Design Kit / upload `design.json` + previews
3. Submit for review (usually &lt; 72h)
4. Promote your creator page & UTM links
5. Get paid weekly (min $25)

## Quality tips

- Optimize for ATS (single-column friendly PDF order)
- Honest previews
- Clear title + category
- Update listings based on reviews

## Rules

- Original work only
- No resale of others’ templates
- Respond to disputes within 48h
