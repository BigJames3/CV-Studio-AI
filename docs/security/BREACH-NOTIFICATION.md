# Breach notification checklist (GDPR Art. 33–34 / CCPA)

Use when confidentiality, integrity, or availability of personal data is compromised.

1. **Awareness timestamp (UTC)** — starts the 72-hour clock
2. Notify DPO / legal (`privacy@cvstudio.ai`, `legal@cvstudio.ai`) within 1 hour of P1/P2 personal-data suspicion
3. Document: nature, categories, approximate data subjects, likely consequences, measures taken
4. If risk to rights/freedoms: notify the supervisory authority **within 72 hours**
5. High risk to users: communicate without undue delay (facts, what we did, what they should do) — no marketing
6. Keep a breach register (Art. 33.5)
7. CCPA: if California residents are affected, follow counsel on AG / consumer notice thresholds

Do not send customer email or press statements without IC + legal.
